System Design Interview

Chapter 1: Introduction
A system design interview evaluates how you approach building scalable systems. You discuss requirements, constraints, high-level architecture, data models, and trade-offs.

Chapter 2: Core Concepts
Scalability can be vertical (bigger machine) or horizontal (more machines). Availability is often measured in nines (e.g. 99.9%). Latency and throughput are key metrics. Use load balancers, caching, and databases appropriate to the use case.

Chapter 3: Example: URL Shortener
Requirements: short links, redirect to original URL, high read volume. Design: generate short code (hash or base62), store mapping in a database, use cache for hot links. Consider rate limiting and analytics.
